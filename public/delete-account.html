Delete your account

Last updated: 17 September 2026
You can delete your KashBook account yourself, at any time, from inside the app. If you no longer have access to the app, you can ask us to do it by email. This page explains both routes, what is deleted, and the records the law requires us to keep. KashBook is operated by Reliant Technology Solutions.

1. Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open KashBook and sign in.
  2. Open the More tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then Continue.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete my account permanently.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You are signed out on every device and your login stops working.

2. Ask us by email

If you cannot use the app, email privacy@relianttechnology.com.ng with the subject “Delete my account” from the email address on your KashBook account, and include the phone number registered to it. We will verify it is you, then delete the account within 7 days and confirm by reply.

3. Before you delete

Money in your KashBook bank account. If the account still holds a balance, deletion is refused until you have transferred the money out. This protects you: once the account is deleted, that money would have no owner we could return it to. Send the balance to any bank account first, then delete.

Your subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel KashBook Pro. It is billed by the App Store or Google Play, not by us, so cancel it there or you will continue to be charged: on iPhone, Settings → Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store app → Payments & subscriptions.

Staff accounts are removed by the business owner from Staff Management, not through this process.

4. What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number and login credentials. Your profile photo is removed from your account.
  • Access for any staff you added: their logins stop working.
  • Your push-notification token, so we stop sending notifications to your device.
  • Any connected Instagram or WhatsApp account is disconnected and its access token deleted.

Your personal details are removed so that the remaining records can no longer identify you.

5. What is kept, and why

KashBook offers bank accounts issued by a CBN-licensed partner, so we are required by Nigerian anti-money-laundering and financial record-keeping rules to retain certain records after an account is closed:

  • Bank-account transaction records (transfers in and out).
  • Identity-verification (KYC) records used to open the bank account.
  • Compliance and audit logs.
  • Your businesses are closed, not deleted: the business name, logo, address, invoice details and your books (sales, expenses, products and their photos, customers, invoices) stay with these records and can no longer be reached from any login.
  • Messages already saved in your KashBook inbox from a connected Instagram account stay with the closed business's records. Email us if you would like them removed.

These are retained for the period the law requires, typically five years, kept encrypted where the data is sensitive, unlinked from your deleted profile, and then deleted. They are never used for marketing.
