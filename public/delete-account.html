Delete your account

Last updated: 14 September 2026
You can delete your KashBook account yourself, at any time, from inside the app. If you no longer have access to the app, you can ask us to do it by email. This page explains both routes, what is deleted, and the small set of records the law requires us to keep. KashBook is operated by Reliant Technology Solutions.

1. Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open KashBook and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You are signed out on every device and your login stops working.

2. Ask us by email

If you cannot use the app, email privacy@kashbook.app with the subject “Delete my account” from the email address on your KashBook account, and include the phone number registered to it. We will verify it is you, then delete the account within 7 days and confirm by reply.

3. Before you delete: money in your KashBook bank account

If your KashBook bank account still holds a balance, deletion is refused until you have transferred the money out. This protects you: once the account is deleted, that money would have no owner we could return it to. Send the balance to any bank account first, then delete.

Staff accounts are removed by the business owner from Staff Management, not through this process.

4. What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number, profile photo and login credentials.
  • Your business profiles, and access for any staff you added.
  • Product photos and other images you uploaded.
  • Your push-notification token and device settings.
  • Any connected Instagram account: the access token is deleted and cached messages removed.
  • Your subscription is not renewed. Manage or cancel it in the App Store or Google Play, which handle billing.

Your personal details are removed so that the remaining records can no longer identify you.

5. What is kept, and why

KashBook offers bank accounts issued by a CBN-licensed partner, so we are required by Nigerian anti-money-laundering and financial record-keeping rules to retain certain records after an account is closed:

  • Bank-account transaction records (transfers in and out).
  • Identity-verification (KYC) records used to open the bank account.
  • Compliance and audit logs.

These are retained for the period the law requires, typically five years, kept encrypted, unlinked from your deleted profile, and then deleted. They are never used for marketing.

6. Questions

Anything about deletion or your data: privacy@kashbook.app. Our full Privacy Policy covers everything else we do with your information.
