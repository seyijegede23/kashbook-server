Support

Reliant Technology Solutions · Lagos, Nigeria
KashBook is bookkeeping and a business bank account for Nigerian small businesses. If something is not working, or you have a question about your account, your money or your data, this is how to reach us.

Contact us

support@relianttechnology.com.ng

Write from the email address on your KashBook account and include the phone number registered to it, so we can verify it is you. We reply within one business day, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm West Africa Time.

Please never send your password, transaction PIN or BVN by email. We will never ask for them.

Opening a bank account

The account is opened from Profile → Bank and is verified with your BVN. Accounts are issued by our CBN-licensed banking partner, and applications are reviewed before an account number is issued. If your application has been waiting more than two business days, email us with the phone number on the account.

Transfers

Every transfer is confirmed with your transaction PIN. If a transfer shows as sent but the recipient has not received it, allow up to 24 hours for the receiving bank, then email us the transfer reference shown in the app. Do not send the money again.

Forgot your PIN? Reset it from Profile → Security; a code is sent to your email.

Subscriptions (KashBook Pro)

Pro is billed by the App Store or Google Play, not by us, so cancellations and refunds are handled there: Settings → Subscriptions on iPhone, or the Play Store app → Payments & subscriptions on Android. If you paid but Pro features are still locked, restore purchases from Profile → KashBook Pro, then email us if it is still locked.

Your data

  • Privacy Policy: what we collect and why.
  • Delete your account: from the app, or by email if you no longer have access.
  • Privacy requests: privacy@relianttechnology.com.ng.

Report a security issue

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in KashBook, email security@relianttechnology.com.ng. We read every report and will not take action against good-faith research.
